### F10.7 age

The 2 hour limit does not fit this feed. It carries three readings a day, stamped 17:00
(Morning), 20:00 (Noon) and 22:00 (Afternoon) UTC, so its newest timestamp is between 0 and
19 hours old on a healthy day. 9.7 h at 07:42 UTC is normal.

`live.py` handles this correctly. `f107_daily` keeps only the Noon reading (20:00 UTC in all
41 days) and stamps it on its UTC day; `build_live_table` forward fills that daily series onto
the hourly index. Checked on today's data: every hour of 2026-11-25 carries 87.0 (that day's
Noon value, the OMNI convention) and the hours of 2026-11-26 so far carry 87.0 forward until
today's Noon reading arrives. No hour is left empty. Treated as ok.

One thing a human may want to know, no change made: the forward fill has no limit, so if the
feed stopped updating the last value would be carried silently for up to the 41 days the feed
holds. F10.7 moves slowly (99 to 87 sfu over the last five days), and the code already calls
it a minor input.
